How to play

Each hole is a scrambled grid. Swap tiles until every row and column holds exactly the atoms of its compound. Order within a line doesn't matter: H, O, H and O, H, H both make Water. What matters is getting each atom into the right row and column. Fewest strokes wins.

🎈Hydrogen🫁Oxygen🍽️Stomach acid 💧WaterHOH 🎈HydrogenHOCl 🧂Table saltNa

Green names are made, gold ones are one tile off. Swapping Cl and H makes Water. Not every swap helps: row 2 slipped further away.

  1. Rows have a compound on the left, columns one on top, by common name only. The formula is up to you.
  2. Tap a tile, then a tile next to it, to swap them (or swipe). Each swap is 1 stroke. Swapping two identical tiles is free and does nothing.
  3. A line is made when its atoms match the formula exactly, in any order, with blank tiles in the leftover cells. Made names turn green; names one tile away turn gold.
  4. Make every row and column at once to sink the hole. Par is the fewest swaps that can do it.
  5. Tap a name for a free clue: how many atoms and blanks, and a fact. Peeking at the formula costs +1 stroke.
  6. Reset tiles puts the board back how it started, but your strokes still count. Stuck? Pick up to see the answer and take par + 5.
  7. Nine holes, from 3×3 to 5×5. Lowest score wins, just like golf.

Everything you make goes in your notebook, with its formula. The Daily course is the same for everyone, and your first finish is the one that counts.
